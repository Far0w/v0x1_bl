Yesterday, I went to see Phenomena, a short film realised by Josef Gatti.
It was a fascinating film in which narrator (which is the director) immerses himself in artistic exploration of scientific phenomenon such as energy, electromagnetism, and light. It wasn't scientifically interesting through accurate but it artistically spot on. It really felt like a tribute to curiosity, to taking the time to observe, and to reconnecting with the world around us. 

The film is beautifully shot and elegantly edited. It draws you into Josef Gatti’s journey of exploration, supported by a mesmerizing ambient soundtrack composed by Rival Consoles. A true pleasure for both the eyes and the ears.

Josef also highlighted the work of Sandbox Films, which seems like a very promising studio for my future thoughtful, science-driven films to explore.

It’s a film I’m adding to my personal collection of pieces of art that help to connect people who are able to see beauty in nature and in the environment that surrounds them. I can only recommend it :)
